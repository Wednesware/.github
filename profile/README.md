## AI Usage and Policy

We use AI **responsibly** in our projects and documentations. We make sure to understand and read through everything AI generates and understand it.

### Why we use AI

Coding takes a long time. Especially in larger projects like Nitrogen or Sodium. AI will **only** generate code we'd already write anyway but much faster.

Documentations generated by AI follow the same principle. It's much faster and nullifies the risk of typos or unclear wording.

Wednesware will **never** use or encourage the use of generative AI models for image, video, music, sound effect or any other sort of media generation, nor will we use AI to automate uploads or posts in any way.
